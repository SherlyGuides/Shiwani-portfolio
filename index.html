    ProjectsSheet03
    Chapter 01 · Public libraries

    American Spaces,
    across Nepal.

    Libraries and maker-spaces for the U.S. Embassy Kathmandu. Reading halls, robotics corners, programming rooms, each one adapted to its city.

    Chapter 02 · Retail network

    Asian Paints,
    one chip at a time.

    Thirteen dealer showrooms from the Terai to the valley. One language of product walls, finishes libraries and consultation counters, tuned to each site. Thirteen rooms, thirteen colours.

    Chapter 03 · Exhibitions & stalls

    Built for three days,
    framed forever.

    Temporary architecture for brands. A stall is drawn in a week, built overnight, survives a thousand visitors and still has to look like a promise.

    Chapter 04 · Homes

    Rooms people
    come home to.

    The shelf where the morning starts. The chair with the better light. The bar where the evening ends. Couples, families, one person and a dog. Everyone has a corner they keep coming back to. I design those first.
